### The Feynman Lectures on Physics

Caltech / Richard Feynman 等 · 物理讲义／在线教材（原课堂讲座整理）

覆盖经典物理、电磁学与量子力学，以解释与直觉见长。适合数学基础较好的读者补充视角，也有人把它作为自学入口。

**适合谁／先修：**建议微积分和初等物理；量子力学卷对初学者更难。

**推荐理由：**stared与alex_smart报告高中自学的正面体验；另有读者认为它不适合作为唯一入门教材，意见分歧应保留。

[资源入口](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/)

**使用备注：**主体是书面讲义，不是完整录像课。读者对入门适合度意见相反；建议配合习题和其他教材。

入口检查：403；自动访问未成功，详见链接检查记录。历史推荐仍保留。

**推荐 · [stared / 22692191](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22692191)**：stared自述高中自学，推荐前两卷并对第三卷作限定。

> I self-studied physics when I was a high-school student. I read The Feynman Lectures of Physics, and it was a great introduction (especially Vol 1 and 2; Vol 3 gives interesting insights but I wouldn't treat is like a canon of quantum physics).

**推荐 · [alex_smart / 31555153](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31555153)**：alex_smart提供与批评者不同的高中自学体验。

> As a counterpoint, I am someone who did self-learn physics from Feynman's lectures as a teenager and I would highly recommend it to others to at least give it a try.

**限制／异议 · [PheonixPharts / 31549421](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31549421)**：PheonixPharts读约三分之一后认为文字优秀但教学效果未必适合自己。

> I've read about a 1/3 of the series and while I have to say they are some of the greatest technical writing in history, they are not the greatest pedagogical material.

### The Theoretical Minimum

Leonard Susskind / Stanford Continuing Studies · 理论物理讲座系列，另有同名配套书

以相对精简的数学框架学习经典力学、量子力学等理论物理，适合已有STEM基础并愿意跟推导的人。

**适合谁／先修：**建议微积分与线性代数；不同子课先修不同。

**推荐理由：**timnic认为讲解基础很容易进入；j_crick喜欢经典和量子力学讲座；gusfrehse特别推荐引入较重数学的量子部分。

[资源入口](https://theoreticalminimum.com/)

**使用备注：**这是多门讲座系列，不是一个带统一评分、证书的MOOC。这里推荐讲座入口，未把配套书和每个子课逐项重复计数。

**推荐 · [timnic / 16746312](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16746312)**：timnic喜欢可进入的讲法，并与自己当年学习物理的经历比较。

> The Theoretical Minimum lecture series on theoretical physics by Leonard Susskind. Covers the basics in a very approachable way. I wish this had been available when I studied physics.

**推荐 · [j_crick / 35539082](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35539082)**：j_crick喜欢经典与量子讲座，同时指出没有人发作业和成绩。

> I really liked The Theoretical Minimum lectures on classical and quantum mechanics by Leonard Susskind (suggestion: google up the guy, he’s cool) at Stanford. You can buy books, but the lectures are all free on YouTube.

**推荐 · [gusfrehse / 34593769](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=34593769)**：gusfrehse推荐量子力学子系列。

> Can vouch for the quantum mechanics one. Perfectly understandable while introducing heavy math machinery.

### Structure and Interpretation of Classical Mechanics

Gerald Jay Sussman、Jack Wisdom 等 / MIT · 可在线阅读的教材＋程序化力学练习

借程序表达并探索经典力学，适合有编程背景、想把拉格朗日力学与可运行模型联系起来的人。

**适合谁／先修：**建议微积分、基本经典力学与函数式编程；历史示例依赖MIT Scheme体系。

**推荐理由：**thatcherc说这本书是自己从CS转向物理的重要原因；PheonixPharts也很喜欢，但提醒实现依赖特定库和Scheme环境。

[资源入口](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/mac/users/gjs/6946/sicm-html/book.html)

**使用备注：**31549597仅略读序言、计划未来学习，所以只用于确认原始链接，不用作完成者推荐。书可自学，但实现环境可能增加门槛。

**推荐 · [thatcherc / 22693088](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22693088)**：thatcherc直接推荐给编程背景者，并叙述转专业影响。

> Structure and Interpretation of Classical Mechanics is so cool - I highly recommend it, especially to someone with a programming background. It's one of the main reasons I switched from CS to physics in college.

**有条件推荐 · [PheonixPharts / 31549665](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31549665)**：PheonixPharts的正面评价附带环境限制。

> I'm a huge fan of that book, but am very glad you called it out as more people should know about it. there's also a great companion book "Functional Differential Geometry". My one caveat with that approach is it requires a very specific implementation of their library in MIT Scheme.

### CFD Python: 12 steps to Navier–Stokes

Lorena A. Barba 等（Barba group） · 开源notebook教程／计算流体动力学入门

用Python和小型数值实验逐步理解流体方程与数值求解，适合希望先从代码连接物理表达的程序员。

**适合谁／先修：**建议Python/NumPy、微积分及基本数值方法；它只覆盖流体这一部分物理。

**推荐理由：**dave_sullivan说在读物理书后仍不明白如何编程实现，而这套教程帮助其理解代码与物理如何接起来。

[资源入口](https://github.com/barbagroup/CFDPython)

**使用备注：**一条明确个人体验；未找到第二条独立推荐。它是流体模拟教程，不是完整物理本科课程。

**推荐 · [dave_sullivan / 22691981](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22691981)**：dave_sullivan报告CFD教程对建立代码与物理联系的帮助。

> But then there's this, you might find it interesting, it helped me understand how everything fits together a lot more:

### Einstein's Relativity and the Quantum Revolution: Modern Physics for Non-Scientists, 2nd Edition

The Great Courses / Richard Wolfson · 面向非专业者的商业视频讲座

由经典物理逐步走向相对论与量子观念，适合以概念理解为主要目标、暂不追求理论物理训练的人。

**适合谁／先修：**适合具备中学科学常识的观众；不是数学推导型物理本科课。

**推荐理由：**freedomben从模糊的中学物理基础出发自学，明确推荐这一门作为开头，因为从经典物理铺垫到爱因斯坦帮助了自己。

[资源入口](https://www.thegreatcourses.com/courses/einstein-s-relativity-and-the-quantum-revolution-modern-physics-for-non-scientists-2nd-edition)

**使用备注：**只有一位推荐者。The Great Courses是商业讲座服务；不把帖子里当年的链接写成今天可免费旁听。此条只计所选这一门，不把同评论另外两门一起计数。

入口检查：403；自动访问未成功，详见链接检查记录。历史推荐仍保留。

**推荐 · [freedomben / 31552897](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31552897)**：freedomben的个人入门体验，限定目标为广泛概念理解。

> This was a great one to start with because he starts with classical physics and works upward through Einstein, which helped me understand so much.

### The Art of Electronics

Paul Horowitz、Winfield Hill；Cambridge University Press · 电子学教材／工程参考书

面向实际电子电路设计，把元件、模拟和数字电路中的常见现实问题讲清楚，适合边做项目边学习的人。

**适合谁／先修：**建议基础电路概念；配合实验、仿真和Learning the Art of Electronics实验手册更合适。

**推荐理由：**kurthr喜欢它补足理想化学术教材缺少的真实问题；zxexz认为它直观而信息丰富；vvanders因实际工程知识深度经常回看。

[资源入口](https://artofelectronics.net/)

**使用备注：**教材与配套实验手册是不同出版物；这里只计主教材一条。评论中的旧实验套件供应有争议，未据此承诺材料包可购。官方书站已核对。

**推荐 · [kurthr / 26526075](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26526075)**：kurthr有电子学实践，喜欢本书的现实问题处理。

> I really liked The Art of Electronics by Horowitz and Hill, which has an associated lab book you could use for little projects. It covers a lot of real world issues that are missing from the more ideal academic books.

**推荐 · [zxexz / 26526211](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26526211)**：zxexz推荐教材，并说明与实验手册搭配。

> I can't recommend The Art of Electronics highly enough - it's way more intuitive and informative than any other resource on the subject I've found. It's exceptionally great when paired with the self-paced lab manual Learning the Art of Electronics[0]. [0] https://www.digikey.com/en/resources/edu/harvard-lab-kit

**推荐 · [vvanders / 26526345](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26526345)**：vvanders认可新版本更新和工程知识深度。

> I'll second this, despite the first version being written over 40 years ago it's still one of the best books I've seen on the subjects. The more recent versions bring it up to date well, it's a dense book but one I find myself coming back to more than any other for the incredible depth of practical engineering knowledge.

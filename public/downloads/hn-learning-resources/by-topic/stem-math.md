### Khan Academy：数学课程与练习

Khan Academy / Sal Khan · 分级视频课程＋在线练习

从算术、代数逐步补到微积分的学习单元，适合离校多年或基础有缺口的成人。可按掌握情况选择起点并反复练习。

**适合谁／先修：**按当前单元分级；建议先查清代数、分数、函数等基础缺口。

**推荐理由：**rectang特别推荐稳定、可拆成小块的练习；komali2说它补上了频繁转学留下的概念缺口。

[资源入口](https://www.khanacademy.org/math)

**使用备注：**这是一组具体数学单元，不是单门大学课。不同分支体验有差异；基础练习不能替代高等数学的证明训练。

**推荐 · [rectang / 16745491](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16745491)**：rectang把推荐重点放在练习设计，而不是只夸视频。

> Khan Academy math, because of the exercises. They are consistent, not very buggy, gamified, and consumable in small or large amounts. Sal Khan is a good communicator and the videos are decent, but it's the exercises that make Khan Academy exceptional.

**推荐 · [komali2 / 16746558](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16746558)**：komali2报告个人补基础的效果。

> Khan Academy filled the gaps from my inconsistent public schooling (moved a lot as a kid). Used to think I was just dumb (I might still be lol), but turns out missing some of the early math concepts is extremely destructive to later learning. Fill the gaps and everything else becomes so much easier .

**有条件推荐 · [alexdowad / 16562503](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16562503)**：alexdowad肯定代数和微积分，但表示在线性代数部分退出。

> I found Khan Academy really helped with high-school algebra, single-variable calculus, and especially multi-variable calculus (the visualizations were great). Khan Academy's linear algebra course was awful and I dropped out there.

**适合谁／先修：**建议熟悉高中代数；可与正式线性代数课同时学习。

**推荐理由：**gringoDan称它对直觉理解很关键；nerusskyhigh说这些视频打开了思路、改善了自己的理解。

### Calculus: Single Variable

University of Pennsylvania / Robert Ghrist；Coursera 托管 · 大学MOOC／微积分系列

从单变量微积分进入泰勒级数等观点，适合重学微积分或想换一种讲法理解概念的人。

**适合谁／先修：**建议函数、三角函数及高中代数熟练。

**推荐理由：**cmsd2赞材料且指出考试并不简单；goose847特别喜欢通过泰勒级数理解微积分，并说课程使自己重新欣赏数学。

[资源入口](https://www.coursera.org/learn/single-variable-calculus)

**使用备注：**2020年评论把链接称作五门系列的第一门；不要把单个入口误写成整个系列全部已完成。

**推荐 · [cmsd2 / 16746664](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16746664)**：cmsd2明确推荐Ghrist的具名单变量微积分课。

> without a doubt: Calculus in a single variable with Robert Ghrist https://www.coursera.org/learn/single-variable-calculus for lots of people here it'll revisit some material you learnt at school but it does go further and the materials are fantastic and the exam at the end is no pushover either.

**推荐 · [goose847 / 22830657](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22830657)**：goose847解释其泰勒级数视角为何吸引自己。

> The reason it can be so challenging and the reason I find it so incredible is that it teaches Calculus through the lenses of Taylor Series.

### Harvard Stat 110 / Introduction to Probability

系统学习概率并训练识别问题结构、对称性等解题方法，适合想建立概率直觉的本科程度读者。可配套Introduction to Probability教材。

**适合谁／先修：**建议高中代数熟练，基础微积分有帮助。

[资源入口](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/stat110/home)

**使用备注：**把书和同一Stat 110课程作为一组资源；不重复计数。不是测度论概率的替代。

**推荐 · [dwohnitmok / 17475986](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17475986)**：dwohnitmok亲自解释为何适合作为第一本概率教材，并指出配套课程材料。
